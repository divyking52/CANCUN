Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to Hospitality Cancún advertising campaign landing pages and qualification forms.

Who we are

Hospitality Cancún coordinates medical tourism inquiries related to Costamed facilities in Riviera Maya and Cozumel, Mexico. This notice covers information collected through paid advertising landing pages and the initial qualification form.

What we collect on the advertising form

We collect only information needed to arrange a consultation:

  • Full name, email, phone or WhatsApp
  • U.S. state
  • Procedure of interest and preferred timeline
  • Whether financing may be required
  • Ability and willingness to travel to Mexico
  • Preferred contact method and time
  • Communication consent and privacy acknowledgment
  • Campaign and traffic source data (for example UTM parameters)

We do not collect detailed diagnoses, medical histories, photographs, or medical records through the standard advertising form or ordinary advertising CRM.

How we use this information

  • To contact you about your inquiry
  • To notify a patient coordinator and schedule a consultation
  • To track campaign performance and lead status in an advertising lead-management system
  • To improve landing page and form experience

Separation from clinical records

Advertising qualification is separate from clinical eligibility. If you proceed, sensitive medical information is collected later through an approved secure medical workflow. Clinical eligibility and treatment decisions are made only by qualified medical or dental professionals.

Sharing

Inquiry details may be shared with assigned patient coordinators and Costamed operational partners as needed to respond to your request. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers that process form submissions (for example webhook or CRM tools you configure) process data under their respective terms.

Consent & communications

By checking the communication consent box, you agree to be contacted about your inquiry by your chosen method. You may opt out of marketing communications by contacting us; transactional messages about an open inquiry may still be necessary to fulfill your request.

Retention

Advertising lead records are retained as needed for follow-up, compliance, and operations, then deleted or archived according to internal policy. Demo-mode submissions stored in your browser localStorage remain only on that device until cleared.

Cookies & conversion tracking

Campaign pages may use analytics or advertising pixels (when configured) to measure page views and form conversions. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

To access, correct, or request deletion of advertising lead data, or to ask privacy questions, contact Hospitality Cancún through hospitalitycancun.com.

Updates

We may update this notice as campaigns or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will change when material updates are published.

This notice is operational guidance for campaign forms and does not replace advice from your legal counsel regarding HIPAA, Mexican privacy law, state privacy statutes, or advertising platform policies.
