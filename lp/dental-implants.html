What dental implants involve

A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth or crown. Many patients travel for planned implant care when they are uninsured, underinsured, or paying out of pocket.

This page describes Costamed’s programmed dental implant packages for medical travelers. A qualified dental professional decides whether implants are appropriate for you after consultation and records review — not through this advertising form.

Questions about treatment & travel

What is included in the dental implant package price?

Published packages typically include doctor’s fees, hospital medicines and materials for the procedure, hotel nights (double occupancy), and airport–hotel transportation. Some packages list a hospital stay night.

Are flights included?

No. Air transportation is listed as not included. You arrange flights; we coordinate ground transfer and hotel per the package.

Can I get financing?

Financing inquiries can be explored through multiple institutions. Approval, rates, and terms are subject to lender review and are not guaranteed.

Will filling out this form diagnose me or approve surgery?

No. This form only qualifies advertising inquiries for a consultation. A qualified medical or dental professional makes all treatment and eligibility decisions.

Do I need to send medical records or photos now?

No. Do not upload diagnoses, histories, photographs, or records on this form. Those are collected later through an approved secure medical workflow if you proceed.

Who is this campaign for?

Uninsured, underinsured, or self-pay U.S. patients exploring planned dental implant treatment in Mexico — subject to clinical eligibility.
